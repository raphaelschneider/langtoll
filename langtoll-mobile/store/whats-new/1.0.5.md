# What's New — 1.0.5

Paste per localization into App Store Connect → 1.0.5 → "What's New in This Version".
Build 55. Follows 1.0.4 within days (2026-10-04): a first screen that shows the app
instead of describing it, the first fare read aloud, every Plus benefit on the plan
screen, notifications asked only where the app explains why, and a smoother
sentence builder.

## en-US
```
• A first screen that shows how LangToll works: your apps behind the shield, a word on the Dynamic Island.
• Your first fare is read aloud, in a native voice.
• The plan screen now lists everything Plus includes.
• Building a sentence: words slide apart to make room for the one you're dragging.
• Notifications are asked for only when the app explains what they are for.
• Bug fixes and polish.
```
