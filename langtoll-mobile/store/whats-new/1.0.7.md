# What's New — 1.0.7

Paste per localization into App Store Connect → 1.0.7 → "What's New in This Version".
2026-10-09 (1.0.7 was never submitted; its language-request line rides along): the first fare is the real thing, and it is on us. Onboarding ends at the
lock; the demo quiz and the subscription screen inside onboarding are gone. Full
access until the first pass runs out (a day at most), then the plan screen. Without a
trial there is no free plan afterwards: the wall is the home screen until one starts
(founder, 2026-10-09). The free plan remains the fallback for a lapsed Plus.
