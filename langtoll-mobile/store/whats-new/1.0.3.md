# What's New — 1.0.3

Paste per localization into App Store Connect → 1.0.3 → "What's New in This Version".
This version carries real features since 1.0.2 (builds 42–45): the night off, the
weekly recap, the first fare inside onboarding, the phone's own language as a
course. The fixes (the onboarding header's empty corner advancing, the paywall
defaults, Tolly jumping in the sentence builder) sit under the last bullet.

## en-US
```
• Take a night off: the lock lifts until 8 in the morning, no need to switch it off.
• Your week in words: a Sunday recap of fares paid and words learned.
• Try a real fare before you decide, right in the setup.
• Learn the language your phone already speaks.
• Bug fixes and polish.
```
