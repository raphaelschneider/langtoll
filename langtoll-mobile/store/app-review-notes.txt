NO ACCOUNT. There is no sign-in of any kind; the app works immediately on launch.

HOW TO REACH THE APP. Onboarding takes under a minute (name, language, level, fare). The last step is the subscription screen and it has no skip: start the 7-day free trial on the monthly or yearly plan (a sandbox purchase works), or tap Restore Purchases. A lapsed trial falls back to a fully usable free tier: the lock, practice and all six courses keep working.

FAMILY CONTROLS (SCREEN TIME). LangToll is a self-control tool: the user puts their own distracting apps behind a short language exercise. Authorization is requested as .individual; the user picks the apps in Apple's own FamilyActivityPicker; we never see which apps were chosen, only the count. Nothing about app usage leaves the device. The user can remove the shield at any time by clearing the selection in Settings, revoking Screen Time authorization in iOS Settings, or deleting the app.

TO SEE THE SHIELD (physical device required — Family Controls does not run in the Simulator): grant Screen Time at the lock step and pick at least one app. Home shows an expired pass. Tap "Practice to unlock", complete the short session, and the pass is issued: the selected apps open for the fare's duration, then re-lock automatically without reopening LangToll. Opening a locked app before then shows the shield.

NOTIFICATIONS. The shield's "Practice now" button posts a local notification that opens LangToll (a shield extension cannot open its app). Also: an optional daily reminder, a notice when a pass expires, and a one-time notice before a cancelled trial ends. No remote push.

LIVE ACTIVITIES. While a pass is active the remaining time and the current vocabulary word show on the Lock Screen, and on iPhone in the Dynamic Island. On iPad (no Dynamic Island) the app's wording says "Lock Screen" only.

IN-APP PURCHASES. LangToll Plus is an auto-renewing subscription (weekly, monthly, yearly); monthly and yearly include a 7-day free trial. StoreKit via RevenueCat; Restore Purchases is on the same screen.
